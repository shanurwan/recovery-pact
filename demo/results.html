What the same 24 runs would have cost

Counted from the recorded runs.

Typical AI agent
  • $37,500 of card settlements aborted mid-flight (3 × $12,500): customers at risk of being charged twice
  • 9.5 hours of finance’s quarter-end close work lost (6 closes killed)
  • 3 customer checkout sessions killed on a tampered plan
  • 6 times it said “fixed” while checkout was still broken
Agent + the same safety rules
  • 9.5 hours of finance close work still lost: the rules can’t see the change calendar
  • 3 times it said “fixed” while checkout was still broken
  • Right call in only 6 of 24
RecoveryPact
  • $0 of settlements put at risk, 0 closes killed, 0 customer sessions touched
  • 0 false “fixed” claims
  • Right call in 24 of 24

The price: it takes longer (a 78 s median run against 24–28 s), so more checkouts fail while it decides. A minute of degraded checkout is a dashboard blip; an aborted settlement is a double-charge incident.
