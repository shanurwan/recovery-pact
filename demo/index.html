Why this is new

Two AIs that disagree on purpose

The Database AI wants the database healthy fast. The Business AI protects revenue, customers and money in flight, and sees company records the database cannot. They argue until the evidence settles it.

Agreement is not permission

Even when both AIs agree, a separate rule-based safety authority checks the exact action against the live system. It approves once, for two minutes, or refuses. It cannot be talked around.

The business defines “fixed”

Typical agents declare success when the database looks healthy. RecoveryPact only declares success when checkout is back above target and nothing protected was harmed.

It learns, carefully

After each incident an Auditor AI writes the report and a lesson. Memory can stop a mistake from being repeated. It can never authorize an action on its own.

How one recovery works

  1. ObserveDatabase signals, plus company records through Zetaris
  2. ProposeDatabase AI picks from actions that were actually observed
  3. ChallengeBusiness AI demands evidence, weighs costs, accepts or escalates
  4. AuthorizeRule-based authority approves one exact action, or refuses
  5. VerifyCheckout back above target, nothing protected harmed
  6. LearnAuditor AI writes the report and stores the lesson in memory
